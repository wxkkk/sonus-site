Sonus Support

An engine sound driven by your own acceleration.

Getting started

  1. Mount the phone in a cradle with the screen facing you. Upright or flat both work.
  2. Pick an engine above the rev ring.
  3. Come to a stop, then hold IGNITION for half a second. Sonus records the mounting attitude during that first half second, so the car needs to be still.
  4. Drive. One tap on the key stops the sound.

Questions

No car right now — can I still hear it?
Yes. Tap "Manual" above the IGNITION key, start, and drive the sound with the four buttons.
The sound arrives after the push.
That is Bluetooth latency. Settings → Output & audio → Latency compensation runs the engine early to cancel it; 150–250 ms suits most car stereos. On a cable or CarPlay, set it to 0.
Acceleration reads on the wrong axis.
If the phone sits sideways or at an angle, gravity alone cannot tell where the car's nose is. Settings → Calibration → step 2: tap Start recording and accelerate in a straight line once.
The speed is wrong, or the revs never settle.
Allow location access. Without GPS the speed is integrated from acceleration alone, which cannot detect a steady cruise.
I started from my Apple Watch and there is no Lock Screen card.
iOS only lets an app open a Live Activity while it is in the foreground, so a watch-started drive cannot put one up. Open Sonus once and the card appears for the rest of the drive.
I bought the unlock and changed phones.
Settings → Purchase & restore → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID. The unlock is a one-time purchase with no subscription.
Does Sonus change anything about my car?
No. It never connects to the vehicle. It only plays sound through whatever audio output you are using.
