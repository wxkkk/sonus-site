Privacy Policy

Sonus · Last updated 14 November 2026

Sonus has no app account or developer-operated server. This policy explains what is processed on your device and how app stores and optional watch controls handle information.

What the app reads, and why

Motion. Sonus reads the accelerometer to tell acceleration from braking. Live readings drive the virtual revs and sound. A recorded trip includes derived acceleration and speed samples stored on your device.

Location. In "In-car" mode, Sonus uses GPS speed to correct its own speed estimate, because an accelerometer cannot detect a steady cruise. Location is used only while the engine sound is running, only on the device, and does not store or send location coordinates. On Android, an active In-car session can continue using GPS speed when the screen is off, with a visible media service when allowed by your device settings. Location is optional. Without it, In-car mode estimates speed from the accelerometer. Manual mode does not use location. You can revoke location access in your device settings.

What stays on your phone

Trips you record — duration, peak G, top speed, 0–100 time and a low-rate trace of the drive — are stored in the app's own storage on the device. They are kept until you remove the app or clear its data. Sonus has no backup service and disables Android cloud backup for its app data.

Settings, calibration and whether you bought the full unlock are stored the same way.

Purchases

Apple processes iOS purchases. Google Play processes Android purchases. Sonus receives the information needed to grant or restore access and stores the unlock status locally. Sonus does not receive payment card details. See the Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy for how the stores handle transaction information. The Android billing library may send Google diagnostic information about billing calls and connections to maintain the service.

Optional watch controls

A paired watch can send start and stop requests and display the active engine, gear and revs. Wear OS uses Google Play services to send session status and commands between the paired devices over Bluetooth or an end-to-end encrypted connection through Google. This does not send location coordinates or the trip log. Remove the watch companion or unpair the devices to stop watch access.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

None. Sonus contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash reporter, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Sonus is designed for vehicle enthusiasts. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this page and the applicable app-store privacy disclosures will say so before that version ships.
